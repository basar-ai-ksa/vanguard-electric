Common Questions

Straightforward answers about permits, timelines, and guarantees.

Do I really need a city permit for a panel upgrade?

Yes. The City of Austin requires an electrical permit anytime the main electrical service is modified. This protects your home and ensures your homeowner’s insurance coverage remains valid in case of an issue. We handle the entire application, drawings, and final inspection.

Will my power be off during a panel upgrade?

Power is temporarily disconnected by Austin Energy in the morning (usually around 8:30 AM). We complete the entire replacement, install the new equipment, and restore power before 4:00 PM on the very same day. We never leave a family without electricity overnight.

What does your 10-year workmanship warranty include?

If any breaker connection, switch, conduit fitting, or wiring installed by our technicians ever loosens or fails due to workmanship within 10 years, we come back and repair it at zero charge to you.
