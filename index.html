Engineering Precision

Why licensed master electricians matter.

Electrical mistakes cause 51,000 residential fires annually. We test every circuit beyond the minimum code requirements before putting our license on the line.

01

Infrared Thermography

We inspect every busbar lug and breaker connection under full electrical load using FLIR thermal sensors to eliminate loose micro-resistances before they cause hot-spots.

02

100% Solid Copper Conductors

We never use cheap aluminum branch wiring. Every circuit run uses premium American-made Southwire Romex and copper grounding grids for lasting structural safety.

03

Lifetime Bonded Signoff

If an installation performed by our team ever loosens, trips erroneously, or fails an inspection, we return and repair it at zero cost for as long as you own your home.
