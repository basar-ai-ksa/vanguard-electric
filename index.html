Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about code compliance, permits, warranties, and emergency diagnostics.

In the City of Austin and Travis County, replacing a main service electrical panel requires an electrical permit and coordination with Austin Energy to disconnect and reconnect the utility meter. Unpermitted work is not only illegal—it can cause homeowner’s insurance claims to be denied in the event of a fire. Vanguard handles 100% of the paperwork, plans, permit fees, and city inspection signoffs with a 100% first-time pass rate.
A distinct fishy or burning plastic odor indicates electrical arcing where plastic wire insulation is melting under dangerous resistance. If safe, turn off the affected breaker or your main breaker immediately, and call our 24/7 direct dispatch line at (512) 894-9102. Do not touch exposed metallic panels if buzzing is accompanied by sparking.
It depends on your current household continuous electrical load (HVAC tonnage, electric water heating, range). A Level 2 EV charger demands a continuous 48A or 50A load, which often pushes a 100A panel beyond the 80% NEC safety ceiling. We perform a formal NEC load calculation before installing. If your panel is at capacity, we can install a smart energy management load shed device or perform a modern 200A upgrade.
If any electrical wiring, breaker connection, conduit bend, or junction box installed by our technicians ever fails due to workmanship or installation defect, we return and repair or replace it at zero cost for as long as you own the property. All equipment is also covered by full manufacturer warranties (e.g., 10-year Eaton/Square D panel warranties).
